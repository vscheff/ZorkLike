## State predicate

The one victory transition is the successful action `post_return` at Address Wall. It requires all of the following, after the action's simulation mark and any hazards:

- Runner has positive health and is still playing.
- `filing.issued` and `filing.bearing_open` are true.
- `transfer.stage == "complete"`, `transfer.remaining == 0`, `transfer.old_link == false`, and `transfer.collapsed == false`.
- `transfer.bed` is east or west and equals `transfer.active_line`. That bed has `spilled == true`.
- The intact genuine `closing_band` was in hand, is now in `address_slot`, and `filing.returned == true`.

End immediately when the predicate holds. A completed transfer alone is observable progress. A ruined return band or permanently unavailable line 19 means victory is unavailable in that run. Never volunteer that diagnosis.

## Ending material

At the wall the return band slides in except for a finger's width. The old pad holds only the kitchen, the steps and their people. Close with Mera or a resident presence (lamp in a window, fresh address or a hand above the slit) if consistent with the state, and a small unmistakable sound of the machine falling to rest. The runner's job is finished. Mark the game complete briefly.

- **East:** The under-arch seats on earth. The low orchard has a line of water over its soil. If the seed stock was moved to the shed roof, night brings the first selection of viable grafts. If Van was warned in time, boots and tools were waiting on high ground. If there was no warning, the crew comes back to find the water line through the beds. Spare residents a casualty unless a specific observed outcome already establishes one.
- **West:** The first discharge coats the old storage level in gray silt. If Iss had four marks' warning, the loading boards were already wheeled to the landing and the crew was gone. Without it, the lowest stored goods are spoiled. The surviving upper terrace and orchard have to send things around by the Span.
- If the rent order was hung publicly, its copied numbers travel with the new address so its old disappearance can be contested. If the ferry list was posted instead, the names carry a second record of residency. Without either there is no public documentary record of the disappearance to bring into the ending.

Death takes precedence over success. Preserve posthumous world changes already written by that last mark. Severe collapse can leave the runner alive above ground free to explore. It is not a victory or an automatic game over.
